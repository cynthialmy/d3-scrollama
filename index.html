About the IMDB score

The IMDB score is a widely recognized benchmark for movie ratings, providing a valuable metric to gauge a film's success. By understanding the factors that influence IMDB scores, filmmakers can better tailor their productions to resonate with audiences and achieve higher ratings.

Let's take a closer look at the IMDB scores, what else contribution to the success of a movie?

Bubble Plot: IMDB Score Against Popularity and Budget

The bubble plot reveals that a director's reputation has a big impact on a movie's success, while budget and cast popularity have a lesser effect. High-budget movies tend to receive only mediocre scores. Casts, even unknown ones, can receive good or bad reviews based on their performances. Overall, this bubble plot sheds light on the complex interplay between the different elements that contribute to a movie's reception, providing us with valuable information for making informed decisions about the movies we watch.
